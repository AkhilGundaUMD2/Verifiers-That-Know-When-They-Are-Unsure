# Literature Matrix: Verifiers That Know When They Are Unsure

| Citation / Year | Paper Title & Link | Core Problem | Methodology | Datasets & Benchmarks | Key Findings | Limitations | Relevance to Project |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Ding et al., 2026 | [WildClawBench: A Benchmark for Real-World, Long-Horizon Agent Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.10912) | Lack of realistic, long-horizon agent evaluation in native runtimes with real tools. | Introduces 60 human-authored tasks running in Docker containers with real CLI agent harnesses. Combines deterministic checks, environment-state audits, and LLM/VLM judges. | WildClawBench: 60 bilingual, multimodal tasks across six categories; evaluates 19 frontier models. | The best model achieved 62.2% overall success. Performance depends substantially on the chosen agent harness. | Limited to single-turn instructions; limited coverage of GUI-heavy and specialized workflows. | Provides long trajectories and side-effect checks for evaluating verifier reliability. Could support testing uncertainty detection and escalation, although uncertainty calibration is not directly evaluated. |
| Hoover et al., 2025 | [DynaGuard: A Dynamic Guardian Model With User-Defined Policies](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.02563) | Standard guardrail models are limited to static, pre-defined harm categories, failing to adapt to highly application-specific, user-defined rules. | Develops DynaGuard, a suite of dynamic guardian models that evaluate responses against arbitrary user policies. Offers both rapid pass/fail detection and interpretable chain-of-thought (CoT) reasoning for actionable agent recovery. | DynaBench (a novel dataset of 40K unique bespoke policies, with 61.5k train and 543 test dialogues); evaluated against BeaverTails, WildGuard, ToxicChat, and Aegis2.0. | Outperforms existing dedicated guardian models on user-defined harms and traditional safety categories. Is competitive with frontier reasoning models but operates with lower latency and cost. | Further research is needed on how to best integrate the model's explanations into multi-agent recovery strategies and assess their impact on human trust. | Highly relevant for verifier design. While it focuses on policy enforcement rather than strict uncertainty, its use of CoT to provide actionable feedback for agent self-correction is a critical mechanism for verifiers handling uncertain or failing states. |
